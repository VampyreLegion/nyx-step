🎸 Finish My Jam

Turns a rough recording — guitar scratch, synth sketch, a hummed beatbox — into a finished song. Everything runs on Nyx's GB10 GPU (ComfyUI → ACE-Step / MiniMax, plus local MusicGen).

1 · Upload & Analyze

Drop in an MP3/WAV/FLAC/OGG/M4A. The server measures the real track and reports:

  • Key — multi-window consensus (CQT + STFT chroma) with confidence %, e.g. A Minor (62%).
  • BPM — librosa onset tempo (an energy-based method used to lock onto double-time; 188 was mis-detected for a true 95).
  • Chords — constrained to the detected key's diatonic set, e.g. Am - C - F - G - Em - Dm.
  • Duration — the true full length (previously clamped at 120 s).
  • All fields are editable before generating.

2 · Backing tracks

  • 3a · ACE-Step instrumental — full arrangement generated from your jam's BPM/key/scale + custom tags.
  • 3b · MusicGen Melody — local facebook/musicgen-melody (musicgen.service, port 8180) that follows your recording's melodic contour. Green/red badge shows service health.
  • 3c · MiniMax Music3 — full song with vocals + lyrics, up to 5 min.

3 · AI Lyrics

Two buttons in the Jam tab:Automatic writes lyrics from your jam's feel (key/BPM/chords injected into the prompt); with a theme it writes about that topic. Streaming preview lands in the text area — review and tweak before singing.

4 · 🎙️ Add Lyrics to My Song (vocals)

The original jam is never regenerated. The lyric-singing job works in two stages:

  1. Generate — ACE-Step sings the lyrics without an audio reference (a reference would put the model into cover mode and nuke the audio-code path, so it could never sing).
  2. Extract + mix — Demucs pulls the vocal stem out of that generation, then ffmpeg layers it onto your exact upload. Output lives in History as jam_vocals_<name>….

Mixdown controls (v5)

ControlWhat it does
VoiceStyle injected into the generation caption (female/male lead, soul, raspy rock, breathy indie, pop, rap, falsetto, opera…). Defaults to Auto.
Vocal level±12 dB gain on the vocal stem right before the mix.
DuckSidechain-compresses the jam under the vocals, so the song pulls back while they sing (ffmpeg sidechaincompress).
TakesQueues 1–3 separate jobs (seed + i). Compare in History and keep your favorite — files are named …_takeN_…. A/B them.
In-key lockDetects the vocal's key; if it differs from your jam's, pitch-shifts the stem (semitones) so the tonic matches before mixing. Skipped when detection confidence is low.
Melody-followResearch toggle, single take only. After pass 1 lands, its mixed track is fed back to ACE-Step in cover mode as the reference, so pass 2 mirrors the melody; those vocals are then mixed onto your original jam as "<song> (melody-follow)". Queued automatically — about twice the wait.

5 · Tips

  • Multiple takes land as separate History cards — pick your favorite by ear there.
  • The mix preserves your instrumental bit-for-bit as the timing base — the song never changes length or tempo.
  • Melody-follow + in-key lock work together: pass 2's vocals are pitch-locked to your jam's key too.
  • If a pass errors, the follow-up is skipped automatically (nothing half-finishes).
