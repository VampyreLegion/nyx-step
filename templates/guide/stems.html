Join Stems — Mix Stems Back Into One Track

What It Does

The Join Stems feature is the reverse of Demucs separation. After splitting a track into its component stems (vocals, drums, bass, other), you can upload them back and mix them into a single audio file. This is useful for:

  • Re-mixing stems with external processing applied to individual tracks
  • Combining stems from different sources into a new composite track
  • Creating a reference mix after adjusting stem levels externally

How To Use It

  1. Go to the Stems tab and click Join Stems
  2. Upload 2–4 stem audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A) into the Stem 1–4 slots
  3. Enter a name for the output file (default: "Joined Stems")
  4. Click Mix Stems — the files are uploaded and mixed server-side
  5. The joined track downloads automatically as an MP3 file

How It Works

All uploaded stems are loaded and padded to the length of the longest stem (shorter stems get trailing silence). They are then overlaid sample-by-sample and exported as a 320 kbps MP3. Volume levels are preserved as-is — no normalisation is applied, so you can pre-adjust stem levels before uploading.

Tips

  • For best results, use stems that share the same BPM and key
  • Pre-adjust individual stem volume in any audio editor before uploading
  • The output filename is sanitised and saved with a _joined.mp3 suffix
  • A minimum of 2 stems is required; 4 stems gives the full Demucs reconstruction
