Organization & Management

Keep your music library organized with these management tools.

Tag Library

Save and reuse your favorite tag combinations:

  • Click 📚 Tag Library in the Style tab
  • Save your current tags as a named preset
  • Click any saved preset to instantly load those tags
  • Build a library of go-to tag sets for different genres

Song Templates

Pre-built project starters in the Generate tab:

  • 5-min Acid House — TB-303, 128 BPM, A Minor
  • Chill Lo-Fi Beat — Rhodes, 75 BPM, vinyl crackle
  • Epic Trance Journey — Supersaw, 138 BPM, euphoric build
  • Industrial Techno Banger — Distorted kick, 132 BPM
  • Jazz Vocal Ballad — Saxophone, 72 BPM, smoky club
  • Ambient Soundscape — Evolving drones, 60 BPM, C Lydian
  • Save your own templates from the Generate tab

Favorites & Notes

Bookmark and annotate your best generations:

  • Click ☆ on any History card to bookmark it
  • Click the bookmarked ★ to add notes (e.g., "needs louder kick")
  • View all favorites in the History tab

Versioning

Track iterations of a song:

  • Save each generation as a versioned snapshot
  • View version history with parameter diffs
  • Restore any version's settings back into the form
  • Compare what changed between iterations

Collections / Playlists

Group related songs into collections:

  • Create named collections (e.g., "EP Tracks", "Best of Month")
  • Add any History item to a collection
  • Reorder items by dragging
  • View collection as a playlist with sequential playback

Auto-Genre Detection

AI-powered genre suggestion:

  • Click 🔍 Auto-Detect Genre in the Style tab
  • AI analyzes your current tags and suggests matching genres
  • Additional style tags are suggested and can be auto-applied

Mood Arcs

Map the emotional journey of your song:

  • Create mood curves with points for different emotional states
  • Presets: Melancholy, Euphoric, Angry, Chill, Dark, Uplifting, Nostalgic, Anxious
  • Save and load mood arc presets
  • Mood arcs help guide AI generation toward your desired emotional arc
