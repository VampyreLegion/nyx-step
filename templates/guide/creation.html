Quick Song Creation

Speed up your workflow with these quick-creation tools.

Batch Generation

Generate multiple variants at once:

  • Set your tags/caption as normal
  • Choose 2, 4, 6, or 8 variants
  • Each variant uses a different random seed
  • Compare results and pick the best one

One-Click Remix

On any History card, click a remix button:

  • 🥁 Remix Drums — New drum pattern, varied percussion
  • ⚡ Remix Tempo — BPM shifted ±5-15
  • 🎵 Remix Key — Transposed 1-3 semitones
  • 🎭 Remix Mood — Random mood variation applied

Voice-to-Prompt

Describe your song idea by voice:

  • Click 🎙 Record Voice Description in the Arrange tab
  • Speak naturally: "upbeat acid house, 130 BPM, squelchy 303"
  • AI transcribes and converts to ACE style tags
  • Tags are automatically applied to your caption

Quick Groove → Song

Turn a Groove Lab pattern into a full song:

  • Create a groove in the TB-303 Groove Lab
  • Export and upload it to the groove library
  • Click Build Song from Groove
  • AI analyzes BPM/key and suggests matching genre, tags, and lyrics

Smart Fill

Auto-fill empty arrangement sections:

  • In the Arrange tab, click Smart Fill on an empty section
  • AI reads adjacent sections and generates bridging content
  • The section is auto-populated with matching tags
