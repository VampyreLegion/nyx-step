Arrange Tab

The Arrange tab is your command center for building complex, multi-layered compositions.

Sections (Arrangement Builder)

Build a song structure by adding section blocks:

  • Intro/Verse/Chorus/Bridge/Outro — Standard song sections
  • Break/Drop/Build — Electronic music transitions
  • Custom — Freeform section for anything else

Each section has its own caption/tags and duration. Drag to reorder. Click Generate All to queue generation for every section sequentially.

Layer Stacking

Generate multiple musical layers independently:

  • Add layers with individual prompts (e.g., "warm bass", "shimmering pad", "tight drums")
  • Adjust volume and pan per layer
  • Click Generate All Layers to create each layer separately

Call and Response

Create musical conversations between two phrases:

  • Write a "Call" prompt and a "Response" prompt
  • Choose an interleave pattern (1:1, 2:1, 1:2)
  • Both parts are generated and can be arranged in the DAW

Dynamics Automation

Draw an intensity curve across your song's duration:

  • Build & Drop — Rising tension then release
  • Wave — Oscillating intensity
  • Crescendo/Decrescendo — Gradual changes
  • Click the canvas to add points, drag to shape

Tempo/Key Transitions

Add markers for mid-song changes:

  • Set bar number, new BPM, new key
  • Choose transition type: sudden, crossfade, or ramp
  • Transitions are stored with your arrangement

Chord Progressions

Click any progression to inject its harmonic tags into your caption:

  • I-IV-V-I — Classic pop/rock resolution
  • ii-V-I — Jazz standard
  • i-VII-III-VI — Aeolian modal feel
  • And 9 more presets covering pop, jazz, cinematic, and rock styles
