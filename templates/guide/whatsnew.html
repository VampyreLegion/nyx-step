🎙️ Add Lyrics to My Song — vocals on your original jam

The Finish My Jam tab now sings. The AI-written lyrics are sung by ACE-Step in reference-free mode (a lego cover path that fed your instrumental through ReferenceTimbreAudio dropped the LLM audio codes, so it could never produce a singer), then the vocal stem is pulled with Demucs and layered onto your exact upload by ffmpeg.   • Voice style picker — female/male lead, soul, raspy rock, breathy indie, clean pop, rap, falsetto, soprano… injected into the caption; defaults to Auto.
  • Vocal level — ±12 dB post-gain on the vocal before the mix.
  • Duck the song under vocals — sidechain compression (sidechaincompress) lowers the jam while the singer is present.
  • Takes / A-B — queue 1–3 takes as separate jobs (seed + i), named …_takeN_…; compare and keep your favorite from History.
  • In-key lock — detects the singer's key and pitch-shifts the stem into your jam's key (asetrate/aresample/atempo chain) when they clash; skipped if detection is too uncertain.

  • Chords — limited to chords diatonic to the detected key (Am - C - F - G - Em - Dm), so the model gets a coherent map.
  • Duration — true full-file length, no more 120 s clamp.

Tagging & Song-Creation Toolkit (v3.10.0)

Eight features focused on better tagging and faster, smarter song creation. They chain together: score a take → apply the suggested fixes on the same seed → A/B compare the results → Insights learns which tags work for you.

1. Smarter Lint Rules

The Lint tab now catches five more issues that quietly hurt generations:

RuleWhat it catches
Lyrics without a vocal tagYou wrote sung lyrics but no female vocal / male vocal / choir — the model will pick a random voice
Sections vs durationEach section needs ~12 s; warns when your section count can't fit the Duration (e.g. 8 sections in 30 s)
Genre / instrument conflictCombinations like acoustic folk + 808 bass or orchestral + distorted guitar
Overlong sung linesLines past ~16 syllables get crammed by ACE-Step — split them
Genre buried lateAttention favours early tokens; put the genre first

2. Quality Score → Fix Suggestions

Click 📊 Score on any finished job. Beyond the 0–10 grade, the score now diagnoses each weak dimension and proposes concrete tag fixes — e.g. a bass-heavy mix suggests adding bright, crisp, airy; a quiet track suggests punchy, powerful.

An ✨ Apply & Retake button appears when there are actionable fixes. It re-generates the track with the same seed and the remedy tags appended — so the only thing that changes is the fix, letting you hear exactly what it did.

3. Seed A/B Compare

Every job card has 🅰 and 🅱 buttons. Set one take as A and another as B to get a side-by-side player panel with the tag differences highlighted. When both takes share a seed, the panel confirms it — so you know the difference comes from the tags, not random variation. The ideal workflow: 🎲 lock a seed, change one tag, generate, compare.

4. History Insights

The History tab has a 📈 Insights button. It mines your own scored generations and shows your best-performing and weakest tags by average quality. A tag has to appear in at least two scored tracks before it shows, so score a handful of takes first to build the picture. This is personalised best-practice from your own data.

5. Artist Profile — Full Apply

In Legion's BioInfusor, an artist lookup's Apply to state now also sets Genre, Key, and Scale (parsed from the artist's vocal range), not just instruments and vocal tags. Key/scale are only applied when the lookup returns them in a recognisable form (e.g. "sings in G major"); otherwise they're left untouched.

6. Auto-Tag from Reference Audio

After analysing a track in the Analyze tab, click 🏷 Suggest Tags. The local Ollama model reads the detected BPM, key, chords, and lyrics and infers genre + style tags, appending them to your prompt. Turns "Analyze → Apply" into a full style starting point, not just the numeric fields.

7. Negative Prompting (Avoid field)

The Parameters tab has a new 🚫 Avoid field. Comma-separated qualities you want to steer away from (e.g. vocals, muddy bass, distortion) are wired into the diffusion model's negative conditioning. More reliable than trying to positively prompt around something you don't want. Experimental — effectiveness varies by model and tags.

8. Golden Presets

The preset list now ships with 16 built-in "Golden" presets — curated, known-good tag combinations per genre (Lo-Fi Study, Synthwave Drive, Epic Cinematic, Jazz Trio Night, Festival EDM, and more). Loading one sets the full state including the right model, steps, and CFG for that style. They can't be deleted, and your own preset with the same name takes priority.

Tips for Combining Them

  • Start from a Golden preset closest to your target, then refine.
  • Run Lint before generating to catch structure/tag problems early.
  • After a take, 📊 Score it and use ✨ Apply & Retake to fix issues without losing the seed.
  • 🅰/🅱 compare the original and the fix to confirm the change helped.
  • Over time, check 📈 Insights to learn which tags consistently score well for you.
